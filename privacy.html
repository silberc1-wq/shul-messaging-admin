Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

CommunityConnect ("we", "us") is a messaging service operated by FC PRO LLC, doing business as Nivolink, that lets organizations such as synagogues, schools and nonprofits ("Organizations") send announcements to their members by text message and phone call.

Information we collect

  • Member information provided by an Organization or by you: name, mobile phone number, preferred contact method (text or call), and the groups you belong to.
  • Messages sent and received through the service, including replies and keywords such as STOP, START and JOIN.
  • Organization administrator information: name, email address and phone number used to sign in and manage the service.

How we use information

We use this information only to deliver the Organization's announcements, process opt-in and opt-out requests, forward member replies to the Organization, keep records of messages sent, and operate and secure the service.

No sharing of mobile information

FC PRO LLC (Nivolink) does not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers that help us operate the service (for example, our telecommunications and hosting providers), and only as needed to deliver messages.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or START to resume. Reply HELP for help. You can also ask your Organization to update or remove your information, or email us at hello@nivolink.com.

Security and retention

Data is stored with access controls so that each Organization can see only its own members. We keep message records for as long as the Organization's account is active or as required by law.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@nivolink.com.
