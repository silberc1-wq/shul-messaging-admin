Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

CommunityConnect ("we", "us") is a messaging service operated by FC PRO LLC, doing business as Nivolink. It lets organizations such as synagogues, schools, nonprofits, community groups and families ("Organizations") send announcements to their members by text message, automated phone call and email.

The Organization decides who gets its announcements and what they say. We process member information on the Organization's behalf, only to run the service for it.

Information we collect

  • Member information, provided by an Organization or by you when you sign up: first and last name, mobile and other phone numbers, email address, preferred way to be reached (text, call or email), the groups you belong to, and, if the Organization adds it, a mailing address.
  • Consent records: how and when you agreed to receive announcements (for example the signup page, texting JOIN, or calling and pressing a key), and the wording you agreed to.
  • Messages and replies: announcements sent to you, your text replies, answers to polls (for example replying 1 or 2), and keywords such as STOP, START, HELP and JOIN.
  • Calls and voicemails: when you call an Organization's number we record which menu options you choose. If you leave a voicemail or an administrator records an announcement by phone or on the website, the recording is stored so it can be played and forwarded.
  • Delivery information: whether a text was delivered, whether a call was answered or went to voicemail, how long a call lasted, and whether an email was sent. We don't track whether texts or emails are opened.
  • Organization administrator information: name, email address and phone number used to sign in and send, plus a log of changes made on the admin website.
  • Signup and billing information for Organizations: organization name, contact details and the details needed to register its number with phone carriers. Payments are handled by Stripe; we don't receive or store card or bank numbers.

How we use information

We use this information only to deliver the Organization's announcements, run polls, process signups and opt-outs, forward replies and voicemails to the Organization, show the Organization delivery reports and message history, register numbers with phone carriers, bill Organizations, and operate, secure and support the service.

No sharing of mobile information

FC PRO LLC (Nivolink) does not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell personal information.

Information is shared only with service providers that help us run the service, and only as needed: our phone carrier and messaging provider (to send texts and calls), our database and hosting providers, our email provider (to send emails), and Stripe (for Organization payments). Address suggestions on the admin website are looked up with an outside map service, and only a ZIP code is sent to get local times such as candle lighting. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Who can see your information

Each Organization can see only its own members. Within an Organization, administrators see only what their permissions allow. Our staff access information only to set up, support or secure an account.

Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to stop, START to resume, HELP for help.
  • Phone calls: call the Organization's number and press 9.
  • Email: click unsubscribe at the bottom of any email.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, ask your Organization, or email us at hello@nivolink.com.

Security and retention

Information is stored with access controls and encrypted connections. We keep member records and message history while the Organization's account is active. When an account closes we delete or anonymize its data within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Opt-out records are kept so that people who said STOP are not contacted again.

Children

The service is meant for adults and for Organizations. A child's number or email should be added only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above. Big changes will be emailed to Organization administrators.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@nivolink.com.
