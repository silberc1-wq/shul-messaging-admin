Part 1: SMS, call and email terms for members

Program description

CommunityConnect is operated by FC PRO LLC, doing business as Nivolink.

CommunityConnect sends announcements from your Organization (for example your synagogue, school, nonprofit or community group) to its members: schedules, event reminders, closures, polls and emergency alerts. Announcements may come by text message (sometimes with a picture), by automated phone call (read aloud or as a recording), or by email, based on your preference.

How you join

  • On your Organization's CommunityConnect signup page, by checking the box to agree, or
  • By texting JOIN to your Organization's CommunityConnect number, or
  • By calling your Organization's number and choosing to sign up, or
  • By giving your contact details to your Organization (for example on a membership or registration form) and agreeing to receive its announcements.

You will receive a confirmation after joining by text. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies by Organization, typically a few messages per week, more during emergencies. Some Organizations send scheduled messages, such as a weekly reminder.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. FC PRO LLC (CommunityConnect) does not charge members for messages.

Replies and polls

Replies you send are forwarded to your Organization. When an announcement is a poll, replying with a number (for example 1 or 2) counts as your answer.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. Reply START to subscribe again. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@nivolink.com. To stop phone calls, call your Organization's number and press 9. To stop emails, click unsubscribe in any email.

Quiet hours

Organizations can set quiet hours when phone calls are held. Emergency announcements may still be sent during quiet hours.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
