Program description

CommunityConnect is operated by FC PRO LLC (Nivolink).

CommunityConnect sends announcements from your Organization (for example, your synagogue, school or nonprofit) to its members: schedules, event reminders, closures and emergency alerts. Messages may be sent by text message or automated phone call, based on your preference.

How you join

  • By texting JOIN to your Organization's CommunityConnect number, or
  • By giving your mobile number to your Organization (for example, on a membership or registration form) and agreeing to receive its announcements.

You will receive a confirmation message after joining by text. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies by Organization, typically a few messages per week, more during emergencies.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. CommunityConnect does not charge members for messages.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. Reply START to subscribe again. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@nivolink.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
