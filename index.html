Questions

The things people usually ask before signing up.

Do members need to install an app?

No. Members get regular texts, phone calls or emails. Admins send from the admin website, or by texting your organization's number.

How long does setup take?

Setup is free. US phone carriers require every organization that sends texts to be registered, and we do that for you. Approval usually takes a few business days. Meanwhile you can add your contacts and groups, and we test everything before you go live.

Who can send messages?

Only the people you approve: website admins, and the phones you list as texters. Each one can be limited to certain groups. You can also require a YES confirmation before a texted message goes out.

What about members who don't text?

Set them to Call and they get a phone call that reads the announcement, or plays your recording. Or set them to Email. Anyone can also call your number to hear the latest announcement.

How do members join or stop?

They can sign up on your organization's web page, text JOIN to your number, or call and press 4. To stop, they reply STOP to a text, press 9 on a call, or click unsubscribe in an email.

What is a credit?

One text uses 1 credit (up to 160 letters, or 70 with Hebrew, emoji or special characters). A picture text uses 3, a phone call uses 1, and email is free. Contacts are unlimited on every plan.

Can we see who got the message?

Yes. The delivery report shows which texts were delivered, which calls were answered, how long people listened, and who replied. Phones don't report whether a text was opened.

Is it only for organizations?

No. Families use it too, to keep siblings, parents and cousins in the loop with one number.

What happens if we pause?

Tell us and we'll hold your number and your data for a small yearly fee, until you're ready to send again.
