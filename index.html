Questions

The things people usually ask before signing up.

Do members need to install an app?

No. Members get regular text messages, or a phone call if they prefer. Admins send by texting your organization's number, or from the admin website.

How long does setup take?

US phone carriers require every organization that sends texts to be registered. We handle the registration for you. Approval usually takes a few business days, and you can set up your contacts and groups in the meantime.

Who can send messages?

Only the phones you list as texters. Each texter can be limited to certain groups, and every message needs a YES confirmation before it goes out (except EMERGENCY).

What about members who don't text?

Set their preference to Call. When you use SEND, they get an automated phone call that reads the announcement.

How do members join or stop?

Members can text JOIN to your number or sign up on your organization's web page. Anyone can reply STOP at any time and they won't receive texts again.

What happens if we pause?

Choose Keep My Number and your organization's number and data are held for you until you're ready to send again.
